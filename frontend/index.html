OUR PASSION & PHILOSOPHY

The Grill Thrill Story

Grill Thrill began in a student design lab with a simple mission: to rescue comfort food from sterile fast-food monotony. We believe burgers shouldn’t be mass-processed clones wrapped in plastic; they should celebrate house-baked brioche, honest seasonings, crisp local vegetables, and hand-spun sauces.

Every single item on our menu is designed from scratch, tested with genuine food lovers, and served with transparent Indian Rupee pricing. Big cravings, fresh bites, and zero shortcuts.
